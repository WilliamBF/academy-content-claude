# Celonis Academy asset types and constraints

- **Online course:** one or more sections, at least two lessons in total, at least two pages per lesson. Up to about 1.5 hours. Includes hands-on exercises.
- **Microcourse:** pages only, no sections or lessons. Up to about 30 minutes. No hands-on exercises.
- **Standalone video:** a 2-10 minute microlearning nugget.
- **Live webcast:** a quick, on-demand session for a rapid feature rollout.

Check the requested structure against the declared asset type and flag mismatches.
