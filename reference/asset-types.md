# Celonis Academy asset types and constraints

- **Online course:** one or more sections, at least two lessons in total, at least two pages per lesson. Up to about 1.5 hours. Includes hands-on exercises.
- **Microcourse:** pages only, no sections or lessons. Up to about 30 minutes. No hands-on exercises.
- **Standalone video:** a 2-10 minute microlearning nugget.
- **Live webcast:** a quick, on-demand session for a rapid feature rollout.

Check the requested structure against the declared asset type and flag mismatches.

## Metadata limits and defaults (all asset types)

Set these at kickoff, not after the content exists.

- **Title:** 50 characters or fewer.
- **Description:** 75 characters or fewer (shown in the catalog; the longer SEO text is metaDescription).
- **metaTitle:** `<Title> | Celonis Academy`.
- **metaDescription:** 155 characters or fewer.
- **SKU:** 36 characters or fewer.
- **Keywords:** titles and descriptions help search and AI engines find the asset, so include the subject keywords a learner would search for. Never pad text to reach a character count.
- **Standalone video defaults:** duration under 30 minutes; completion criteria "view 90% of the video".
